# Evaluate a pinned candidate

Read [project context](../../references/project-context.md) and
[skill evaluation](../../references/skill-evaluation.md). Define the decision the
trial must support, candidate/control identities, supported clients and practical
side-effect/time limits. Keep evaluation artifacts in an isolated workspace.

Choose representative and held-out tasks, positive/negative triggers, direct
invocation and realistic failure/resume cases. Compare with current skills and a
no-skill/solo baseline where meaningful. Use independent forward agents when
available and authorized, giving raw tasks/artifacts without the desired answer.
Otherwise record the independence limit and complete the useful checks available.

Observe actual installed skills in each claimed native client. Verify selection,
optional-package coexistence, two distinct pins and update/recovery/removal behavior.
Inspect resulting code/artifacts, evidence accuracy and complete delivery. No
manifest, copied body or model label can stand in for those observations.

Measure total coordinator/worker usage, elapsed time, retries, review rounds and
corrective user interventions where exposed. Mark missing telemetry unavailable.
Creative-level comparisons should vary creative output while preserving rigor and
scope. Do not use added agents or reviews merely to inflate apparent rigor.

Diagnose failures, make narrow evidence-backed corrections and repeat affected
cases without erasing negative results. Deliver a promotion, limited-rollout or
rejection recommendation with candidate/client identities, quality/cost comparison
and explicit untested environments or behaviors. A passing structural suite alone
cannot support a release-ready claim.
